Why the flag uses *in deck*: *drawn* and *played* over-count expensive cards. A card is only drawn (and played) in the games that last long enough for it, and the long games are, most likely, the ones the slower decks holding the expensive cards win more often; so a 6-9 mana card reads several points higher drawn than in deck, and its drawn rate hardly moves when its stats change. *In deck* has no such lean, but a smaller spread, since a player draws only about 70% of the deck in a 25-turn game: its band is the old 5 points on *drawn* times 0.7.
